About

My path into data science started with an engineering degree in Mumbai and a stint on an aero design team, where I wrote simulation code for fixed wing aircraft and we placed third globally at SAE Aero Design. That experience taught me that good analysis only matters if it survives contact with the real world.


Since then I've worked across the stack: building web applications at an insurance company, tuning database queries on large confidential datasets, training forecasting models at a software consultancy, and now digging through more than 40 weeks of food waste and purchasing data at UMass to help the dining program cut costs. Along the way I've picked up a habit of caring about the unglamorous parts: pipelines, validation, reproducibility, and dashboards people actually use.


Right now I'm focused on machine learning systems. My recent projects cover credit risk classification, large scale ETL for Reddit data, and a real time anomaly detection engine that's currently in the works.
